GetTranscript

Keep the transcript. Keep the speaker names.

Export in three steps

  1. Open a video page and select GetTranscript in your browser toolbar.
  2. Choose a language and format. Available speaker labels are matched automatically.
  3. Select Download. The file goes to your browser’s downloads folder.

Choose your format

VTT
Web captions with precise timings and standard speaker tags. Enable “Show names in captions” to display the names as text in players that hide voice tags.
SRT
Subtitles for most video editors. Speaker names are included as visible text when enabled.
Text & Markdown
Readable transcripts with timestamps, suitable for notes and documents.
JSON
Structured cue data with start/end times in seconds and optional speaker names.

Supported pages

Microsoft Stream video pages on SharePoint, including standard English and French transcript panels. Other HTML5 players work when they expose readable VTT or native caption tracks. Some sites, closed shadow players, live rolling captions and cross-origin embeds are not supported.

If no captions appear, enable them in the player and refresh GetTranscript. Open embedded videos on their original page. GetTranscript does not transcribe audio or infer who is speaking.

Speaker names

Names come from existing caption tags or the meeting’s transcript labels. GetTranscript matches complete utterance text and timestamps. It leaves unmatched captions unnamed. It does not correct the original service’s attribution errors.

Your privacy

Transcripts are processed in the popup’s memory and saved only when you download them. Only your format and speaker preferences are stored locally. There are no analytics, cloud uploads, developer servers, account registration or AI services.

Temporary active-tab access is granted when you open GetTranscript. The extension may load caption resources referenced by that page, briefly reveal captions or scroll the transcript, and then restore those controls. Existing sign-in, server permissions and content availability still apply. Do not export content you are not authorized to keep.

Permissions

activeTab + scripting: read captions from the page you select. downloads: save your chosen file. storage: remember export preferences. No persistent website access, cookie access, browsing history or debugger permission.
