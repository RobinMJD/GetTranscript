GetTranscript

Keep the transcript. Keep the speaker names.

Version 1.3.1 is a local test build awaiting your approval before Store upload or public release.

Export in three steps

  1. Open a video page and select GetTranscript in your browser toolbar.
  2. Choose a format. A Caption track selector appears only if multiple separate tracks are available. Speaker names are preserved when the recording provides them.
  3. Select Download. The file goes to your browser’s downloads folder.

Close it. Come back when you’re ready.

Reading and exports continue when you close the popup. Reopen it on the same tab to see the current job, completed transcript or download status. Refresh starts a new scan. Closing the video tab clears its cached result. Reopening the popup after navigating to another recording checks its identity and reads that recording.

Combine a meeting’s recordings

  1. In the popup, open More options → Combine recordings. Add up to 20 links from the same website as your source tab, or choose Find links on source to select Stream links exposed by the page. GetTranscript does not guess filenames.
  2. Check the order and choose a caption track if several are available, then select Read recordings. Keep the source video tab open: collection visits each recording there and then restores its starting page and player state, unless you have navigated elsewhere.
  3. Choose One combined transcript in VTT, SRT, text, Markdown or JSON, or Separate files (.zip) with each recording’s original timestamps.

You can close this workspace while reading continues and reopen it from the popup. Use Pause and Continue reading to resume progress, or Retry a failed part. Each part must finish or be explicitly removed before export. Refresh collection keeps its links, order and options while clearing captured content for a fresh read.

Slow Stream players get up to 45 seconds to expose their controls and captions. Supported Transcript controls open automatically. If a part still needs attention, choose Open recording, select Transcript on that page, then return here and choose Retry this part. Retry reads an already-open recording without reloading it. Previously ready parts remain saved.

Progress tells you whether GetTranscript is waiting for the source tab, opening a recording, reading captions or restoring the starting page. Pause cancels a collection that is still waiting for the tab immediately; an active step finishes safely before pausing. A new collection starts with your saved popup format and speaker preferences. You can change the export format while reading continues; your choice is used for download.

Under Advanced options, Join end to end uses full video durations, including silence; it does not infer gaps between recordings. Set start times accepts HH:MM:SS.mmm or seconds to preserve known gaps without overlapping parts. Keep each part’s original times is available for combined Markdown, text and JSON. For subtitles with original times, choose separate files.

Names are resolved separately within each recording. Spoken languages can change within or between recordings without blocking combination; the original text is preserved without translation. Markdown, text and JSON can include links to the recordings. Combined JSON uses schema version 2 with part metadata, local cue times and timeline offsets.

Caption endings can run slightly past a video's reported duration. Differences up to 5 seconds show a timing warning while preserving all captions and their original timestamps. The next part still starts after the reported video duration. Larger differences require refreshing the recording or exporting with original timestamps; separate files remain available.

Choose your format

VTT
Web captions with precise timings and standard speaker tags. Enable “Show names in captions” to display the names as text in players that hide voice tags.
SRT
Subtitles for most video editors. Speaker names are included as visible text when enabled.
Text & Markdown
Readable transcripts with timestamps, suitable for notes and documents. Markdown keeps each bold timestamp and speaker directly above its caption, with one blank line between caption blocks.
JSON
Structured cue data with start/end times in seconds and optional speaker names.

Supported pages

Microsoft Stream video pages on SharePoint. Languages are discovered dynamically from the supported player, with no language whitelist. The interface stays in English; captions keep their original language. Other HTML5 players work when they expose readable VTT or native caption tracks. Some sites, closed shadow players, live rolling captions and cross-origin embeds are not supported.

On Stream, GetTranscript first reads the recording's available transcript and speaker names directly, without scrolling through the transcript panel. If that is unavailable, it reads the player's captions and panel instead. A transcript with several spoken languages stays together; the interface does not assign one language to the conversation.

If no captions appear, enable them in the player and refresh GetTranscript. Open embedded videos on their original page. GetTranscript does not translate, transcribe audio or infer who is speaking. Browser settings and extension Store pages cannot provide a transcript; open the video page first.

Speaker names

Names come from the recording's transcript data, existing caption tags or the meeting’s transcript labels. Panel labels require a complete text-and-time match. Captions without a reliable name remain unnamed. GetTranscript does not correct the original service’s attribution errors.

When the player fallback is needed, long transcript rows are read in resumable steps of about 18 seconds, restoring controls and scroll position between steps. Some players stop loading further rows while their tab is in the background; incomplete speaker coverage is shown in the result. An active capture has a 10-minute budget; continuing after a pause starts a fresh active budget. Collection and text-size limits are reported, and unmatched captions remain unnamed.

Your privacy

Per-tab transcripts, collections, recording URLs, progress checkpoints and job status stay in temporary browser session memory. Refresh replaces cached content; closing the source video tab clears its results and collection. Browser restart or extension reload/update also clears sessions. Only format and speaker preferences are saved persistently by the extension. There are no analytics, cloud uploads, developer servers, account registration or AI services.

Temporary active-tab access is granted when you open GetTranscript. The extension may read the current recording's transcript data or referenced captions, briefly reveal captions or scroll the transcript, and then restore those controls. Existing sign-in, server permissions and content availability still apply. Authentication values are never saved in extension storage or included in exports. Do not export content you are not authorized to keep.

Permissions

activeTab + scripting: read captions from the page you select. downloads: save your chosen file. storage: remember export preferences and temporary per-tab results and collections. No persistent website access, cookie access, browsing history or debugger permission.
