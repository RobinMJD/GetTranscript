GetTranscript

Keep the transcript. Keep the speaker names.

Export in three steps

  1. Open a video page and select GetTranscript in your browser toolbar.
  2. Choose a format. The detected language is shown automatically; a selector appears if multiple caption tracks are available. Speaker labels are matched automatically.
  3. Select Download. The file goes to your browser’s downloads folder.

Close it. Come back when you’re ready.

Reading and exports continue when you close the popup. Reopen it on the same tab to see the current job, completed transcript or download status. Refresh starts a new scan. Closing the video tab clears its cached result. Reopening the popup after navigating to another recording checks its identity and reads that recording.

Combine a meeting’s recordings

  1. In the popup, open More options → Combine recordings. Add up to 20 links from the same website as your source tab, or choose Find links on source to select Stream links exposed by the page. GetTranscript does not guess filenames.
  2. Check the order and each part’s caption language, then select Read recordings. Keep the source video tab open: collection visits each recording there and then restores its starting page and player state, unless you have navigated elsewhere.
  3. Choose One combined transcript in VTT, SRT, text, Markdown or JSON, or Separate files (.zip) with each recording’s original timestamps.

You can close this workspace while reading continues and reopen it from the popup. Use Pause and Continue reading to resume progress, or Retry a failed part. Each part must finish or be explicitly removed before export. Refresh collection keeps its links, order and options while clearing captured content for a fresh read.

Under Advanced options, Join end to end uses full video durations, including silence; it does not infer gaps between recordings. Set start times accepts HH:MM:SS.mmm or seconds to preserve known gaps without overlapping parts. Keep each part’s original times is available for combined Markdown, text and JSON. For subtitles with original times, choose separate files.

Names are resolved separately within each recording. To combine different caption languages, explicitly enable Allow different languages; the text is not translated. Markdown, text and JSON can include links to the recordings. Combined JSON uses schema version 2 with part metadata, local cue times and timeline offsets.

Choose your format

VTT
Web captions with precise timings and standard speaker tags. Enable “Show names in captions” to display the names as text in players that hide voice tags.
SRT
Subtitles for most video editors. Speaker names are included as visible text when enabled.
Text & Markdown
Readable transcripts with timestamps, suitable for notes and documents. Markdown keeps each bold timestamp and speaker directly above its caption, with one blank line between caption blocks.
JSON
Structured cue data with start/end times in seconds and optional speaker names.

Supported pages

Microsoft Stream video pages on SharePoint. Languages are discovered dynamically from the supported player, with no language whitelist. The interface stays in English; captions keep their original language. Other HTML5 players work when they expose readable VTT or native caption tracks. Some sites, closed shadow players, live rolling captions and cross-origin embeds are not supported.

If no captions appear, enable them in the player and refresh GetTranscript. Open embedded videos on their original page. GetTranscript does not translate, transcribe audio or infer who is speaking. Browser settings and extension Store pages cannot provide a transcript; open the video page first.

Speaker names

Names come from existing caption tags or the meeting’s transcript labels. GetTranscript matches complete utterance text and timestamps. It leaves unmatched captions unnamed. It does not correct the original service’s attribution errors.

Long transcript rows are read in resumable steps of about 18 seconds, restoring the player’s controls and scroll position between steps. An active capture has a 10-minute budget; continuing after a pause starts a fresh active budget. Collection and text-size limits are reported in the result, and unmatched captions remain unnamed.

Your privacy

Per-tab transcripts, collections, recording URLs, progress checkpoints and job status stay in temporary browser session memory. Refresh replaces cached content; closing the source video tab clears its results and collection. Browser restart or extension reload/update also clears sessions. Only format and speaker preferences are saved persistently by the extension. There are no analytics, cloud uploads, developer servers, account registration or AI services.

Temporary active-tab access is granted when you open GetTranscript. The extension may load caption resources referenced by that page, briefly reveal captions or scroll the transcript, and then restore those controls. Existing sign-in, server permissions and content availability still apply. Do not export content you are not authorized to keep.

Permissions

activeTab + scripting: read captions from the page you select. downloads: save your chosen file. storage: remember export preferences and temporary per-tab results and collections. No persistent website access, cookie access, browsing history or debugger permission.
